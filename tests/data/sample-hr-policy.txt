Contoso Corp HR Policy - Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) annually.
PTO accrues monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year.
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.
Unused PTO above the carryover limit is forfeited at year end unless otherwise required by local law.

For questions contact hr@contoso.com.
